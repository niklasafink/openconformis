# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must contribute to a professional, harassment-free environment. Be
respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good faith while evaluating evidence,
and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized or personally identifying
content. Regulatory and security disagreements must focus on sources, tests and
risk—not on individuals.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@conformisgrc.com`. The maintainer may
edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or permanently ban a person
when necessary to protect contributors and users. Reports are handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to legal duties.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when a participant officially
represents the project.
